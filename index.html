Cosmic Word Match — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

Cosmic Word Match is a word puzzle game made by Handsome Git Lab ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with information. Contact: handsomegit3@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
  • Your progress is saved on your device only.
  • Test builds include a feedback tool. It keeps notes about your play on your device, and sends them only when you choose to with the SEND RESULTS button, by an app you pick (usually email).

What is stored on your device

  • Your game progress: levels, stars, coins, tools, Starbase, daily rewards and settings. This never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Test builds only: the feedback tool

While the game is in testing, it includes a feedback tool (the REPORT button and the BETA TESTING page in Settings). It records on your device:

  • a random ID for this installation, and a nickname if you choose to type one;
  • your device model, Android version, graphics chip and screen size;
  • how each level went: the result, time taken, moves, the words you spelled, your rating, and the frame rate;
  • the reports you write and the screenshots you choose to take with the REPORT button;
  • the game's log and any errors.

None of this is sent automatically. When you tap SEND RESULTS, the game:

  1. puts these notes in a zip file;
  2. saves a copy in Downloads/CosmicWordMatch on your device;
  3. opens your phone's share menu, so you can pick an app (for example email) and send it to us.

You can open the zip to see exactly what is in it before you send it.

Why: to find bugs, to see which levels are too hard or too easy, and to make the game better.

Who sees it: only us. We do not sell it or share it with anyone.

How long it is kept: until testing ends, then no longer than 12 months.

Deleting it: email us and we will delete what you sent. On your device, uninstalling the game deletes its notes; the zip in Downloads is a separate file you can delete yourself.

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
